CINEMATIC TIMELINE

One Story · Nine Chapters

Scroll down to experience our complete 5-year journey step by step.

CHAPTER 01
30 NOVEMBER 2020 · AMBIKA MARRIAGE HALL

He saw a girl. She kept eating.

He was watching from across the room. She didn't even care that he was there, completely focused on her plate in her lehenga. When she left, he tried following the way back to her house — but lost sight in the crowd.

“That should have been the end of the story. But fate had other plans.”
CHAPTER 02
DECEMBER 2020 · THE FRONT DOOR

The Door & “Mil Gayi Ladki”.

He came over to her home with his friend, planning to describe the mysterious girl he'd seen to her brother. Then the door swung open. Jeans, shirt, casual top, and a subtle smile he tried so hard to hide.

He whispered "Yes, mil gayi ladki." She still had no idea what was happening.
CHAPTER 03
EARLY 2021 · SCHOOL DAYS & BALLYTIA MORNING MEETS

School Uniforms, Ballytia & “O Hero”.

Her brother walked up to him and called out "O hero!" Neither knew they were about to become great friends. We used to meet in Ballytia, a medical place, coming early in the morning in school uniforms just to see each other before classes started.

Plot twist: Two guys competed, but she chose Utkarsh.
CHAPTER 04
MID 2021 · SNAPCHAT, BUNKED COACHINGS & #JANAM

Bunking Coaching & “#JANAM”.

A friend request. A little confusion. Bunking coaching classes just to steal precious moments together. Then writing #JANAM... ♥ in ink on his arm. Good morning, Good night, 11:11 wishes. Talking became an indispensable habit.

CHAPTER 05
16 OCTOBER 2021 · THE CONFESSION & RED ROSE WALL

“So, tell me.”

He gave her an ultimatum: tell me if you feel something real, or we stop talking today. She didn't give a long speech. She simply replied:

“इतना भी नहीं समझते बुद्धू, हम भी आपसे प्यार करते हैं।”
And suddenly, our story had a date: 16·10.
CHAPTER 06
FEBRUARY 2022 · HOME DINNER DATE & SECRET LETTERS

Surprise Home Dinner Date & Diary.

When talking on phones was forbidden, a handwritten diary became their bridge. And when they could meet, Pari planned an unforgettable romantic dinner date at home with candle lights and heart decorations made by her own hands.

CHAPTER 07
05:00 AM · MORNING TWILIGHT & SOFTEST MOMENTS

The Terrace & Softest Moments.

Waking up at 5:00 AM for quiet terrace meetings. Holding cheeks, resting heads, and falling asleep together — the softest, coolest, most peaceful moments of our 5 years.

CHAPTER 08
2023 - 2024 · LONG DISTANCE & VIDEO CALLS

Same people. Different screens.

School ended, studies moved to different cities. Late-night video calls, funny dog filters, pointing at phone screens ("Ur pati 🌻"), and silly arguments — all because they cared so much and were learning how to hold on across miles.

CHAPTER 09
PRESENT DAY · GALGOTIAS COLLEGE

Came to Galgotias for Him.

Both worked hard, cleared JEE Mains & Advanced, and Pari came to Galgotias College Board because of him! Walking side by side on campus road, trips to Vrindavan, ISKCON Noida, and Bobi's café dates — building our shared future together.
